## Source details requiring deliberate treatment

These are witness facts, not automatic corrections:

1. Pages 42, 48, and 49 print function type declarations with a `mapsto` arrow
   where a domain-to-codomain arrow would ordinarily be expected. Preserve the
   printed symbol in the witness; normalize it only through the existing
   adverse/correction process.
2. Page 45 says, as intuition, that spheres within `C` are mapped onto spheres
   within `A C`. A general linear map need not map a sphere to a sphere; the
   theorem is supported by the subsequent sequence argument, so a derivative
   should not silently strengthen that intuition under the source author's
   voice.
3. Page 46's two counterexample rows serve different failures: the first shows
   why the relative-interior qualification is needed, and the second shows why
   closure need not commute with intersection. Keep both rows and their strict
   versus non-strict inequalities distinct.
4. Page 48 proves continuity first at zero and then states extension over
   `ri(dom(f))`; the final extension is a source statement, not an exercise.
5. Page 49 uses a checked closure symbol for the convex closure. Do not flatten
   it into the ordinary closure symbol when retyping the formulas.
