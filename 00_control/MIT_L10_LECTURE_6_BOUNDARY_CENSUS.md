## Content closure

1. Page 64 states the four-part lecture program.
2. Pages 65-68 reduce attainment, linear-image closedness, vector-sum
   closedness, and partial minimization to nonemptiness of nested closed-set
   intersections and projection of epigraphs.
3. Pages 69-70 give the partial-minimization counterexample, visualization,
   and compact-sublevel theorem.
4. Pages 71-76 move from the compact case to recession and lineality through
   asymptotic sequences, retractivity, and two set-intersection theorems.
5. Pages 77-80 apply the refined theory to linear/quadratic programming,
   closure under linear maps, the necessity of retractivity, and closedness of
   vector sums.
6. Pages 81-85 define hyperplanes and halfspaces, distinguish separating,
   supporting, and strict separation, and state/prove the supporting,
   separating, and strict-separation theorems.

No source-order topic is left open at page 85. Page 86 begins a new lecture;
it is not a continuation slide for the strict-separation proof.
