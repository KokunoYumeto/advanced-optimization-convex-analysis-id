### Content progression

2. Pages 30-31: the first-order characterization of differentiable convex
   functions and two diagrammatic proof ideas.
3. Page 32: the variational first-order optimality condition and proof.
4. Page 33: existence, uniqueness, and characterization of Euclidean projection
   onto a nonempty closed convex set.
5. Page 34: Hessian tests for convexity and strict convexity.
6. Page 35: convex combinations, convex and affine hulls, nonnegative
   combinations, and generated cones.

   the minimal-representation/lifting proof.
8. Page 38: compactness of the convex hull of a compact set and the warning that
   a closed set can have a nonclosed convex hull.
