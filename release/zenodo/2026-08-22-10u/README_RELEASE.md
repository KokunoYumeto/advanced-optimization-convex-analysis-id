Version: checkpoint-2026.08.22-10u

This is an explicitly incomplete preservation release, not the completed O015
course. It contains the ten admitted optional-companion reader units available
at this boundary:

- Andreas Habring, *Lecture Notes: Convex Optimization*, Chapters 3-9;
- Christopher Griffin, *Nonlinear Programming*, Chapters 3-5, with
  contributions credited to Simon Miller and Douglas Mercer.

The ten PDFs are uploaded separately for direct reading. The deterministic
source/backend ZIP contains their editable TeX closure, required figures and
bibliography inputs, the 1,283-record modular JSONL/CSV backend, component
rights and source authority, the correction ledger, and bounded QA evidence.
The release manifest and SHA256SUMS bind the exact uploaded bytes.

The selected primary D90 course is MIT OpenCourseWare 6.253 plus Clément
Royer's stochastic-gradient material. That primary reader, semantic HTML/EPUB,
tagged PDF, complete mastery/solution layer, and independent human Indonesian
review are not present in this checkpoint. Penn Chapter 6 and later candidates
are also excluded. GitHub's provider-side suspension does not alter this
Zenodo concept lineage.
